Step 3 mockup, schedule-agnostic. Invented students. Tap anything. Nothing is saved.
1Day total against 6:30, split into codes and notes. Green at 6:30, amber over 9:00.
2Finish the day: your usual activities as chips, sized to the gap. Green closes it exactly. Nothing is added without a tap.
3Roster: alphabetical, every student, with search and a Coded / Not yet filter. Each row has the suggested code, A, and the full picker. A opens the comment box.
4Notes: her usual activities as pills at the top. Tap adds it to today; hold to edit, pin or remove; + New pill saves a new one with its usual time. Hide collapses the row. Pills carry across periods.
5Chart: the grid she already uses, kept as is. Sticky names and dates, tap a cell on the selected day to code it, comments at the right. Tap any day's Notes cell to open that day's notes and add from your pills. The Total row is green at 6:30 or more, glows red on any day that logs under 6:30, and turns red text over 9:00.
6Review: what Donna would ask about, before you export. Red blocks export.
